## Chat-style dialogue

Lines formatted as `Speaker: text` render as a conversation:

Abbott: Strange as it may seem, they give ball players nowadays very peculiar names.

Costello: Funny names?

Abbott: Nicknames, nicknames. Now, on the St. Louis team we have Who's on first, What's on second, I Don't Know is on third--

Costello: That's what I want to find out.

Abbott: I'm telling you. Who's on first.

Costello: I DON'T EVEN KNOW WHAT I'M TALKING ABOUT!

Abbott: Don't get excited. Take it easy.

## Link posts

Link posts point the title at an external URL. Add `link:` to front matter on a separate post:

```yaml
---
title: "Post: Link"
link: https://github.com
---
```

The post title becomes a link to that URL. Useful for sharing a link with a short commentary body.
